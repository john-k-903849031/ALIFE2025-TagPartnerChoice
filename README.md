Erratum 05/06/2026: 
Effect size is inaccurately reported in two sentences:
1.	“Mutualists consistently have less tag mismatch than parasites; this effect is medium or large in all cells other than permissiveness 0.125 and mutation 0.0001 (δ ≈ 0.1, less than small effect) and permissiveness 0.5625 and mutation 0.1 (δ ≈ 0.15; small effect).”
a.	Effect size is also small under the following combinations of parameters:
i.	Permissiveness 0.625, mutation 0.05 (δ ≈ 0.25)
ii.	Permissiveness 0.625, mutation 0.1 (δ ≈ 0.14)
2.	“At larger mutation values (> 0.01) the effect is medium or small except at permissiveness 0.625 and mutation 0.1, which has a negligible effect (δ ≈ 0.105).”
a.	Effect size is large under the following combinations of parameters:
i.	Permissiveness 0.4375, mutation 0.05 (δ ≈ 0.47)
ii.	Permissiveness 0.375, mutation 0.05 (δ ≈ 0.57)
A full spreadsheet of calculated statistics can be found in the stats/ directory.
